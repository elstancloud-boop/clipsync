🔖 Attach from ClipboardPortal

  1. Computer: drag the black button onto your bookmarks bar (Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+B shows it).
  2. Android (Firefox): tap Copy bookmark below, then ⋮ menu → Bookmarks → add a bookmark named "ClipboardPortal" and paste the copied text as its address.
  3. Use it: open any site's upload page, tap the bookmark, tap a clip.

Some sites (banks, Google, GitHub) block bookmarks like this. There, use drag out or Google Drive.
