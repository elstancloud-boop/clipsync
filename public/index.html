Copy on your phone. Paste on your computer. And back.

ClipboardPortal is a free, private clipboard that works across your devices. Send text, links, photos, music, videos and files between an Android phone, a Windows PC, a Mac or another phone, straight from the browser. No sign-up, no app to install, no cable.

How it works

  1. Open clipboardportal.com on both devices and tap Get started on the first one.
  2. Scan the QR code or type the 6-digit code on the second device. The two are now paired.
  3. Paste, drag or share anything on one device. It shows up on the other instantly. Tap a clip to copy it, or save the file.

Works in every direction

  • Android phone to Windows PC or Mac
  • Windows PC or Mac to Android phone
  • Computer to computer
  • Phone to phone

Why people use it

  • Instant start: no account, no email, no password.
  • Drag and drop files onto the page, or press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V to paste images and text.
  • Android Share menu: install it to your home screen and share from any app straight to ClipboardPortal.
  • Auto-Drop: received files can be saved to your Downloads so they appear in upload pickers under Recent and Downloads.
  • Private by default: clips auto-delete after 30 days and you can wipe everything with one tap.

Frequently asked questions

How do I copy on Android and paste on Windows or Mac?

Pair the two devices with the QR code, then paste or share on the phone. The clip appears on the computer within a second.

Do I need to sign up or install anything?

No. It runs in your browser and starts as a guest. You can save your account with an email later if you want to keep your devices linked.

What can I send and how big can it be?

Text, links, images, audio, video and other files up to 25 MB each.

Is it free and private?

Yes, it is free. We do not sell data or show ads, and clips are deleted automatically after 30 days. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms.

Will my clips appear in the Android file picker?

With Auto-Drop on, received files are saved to your Downloads and show under Recent and Downloads in any upload picker. A website cannot register itself as a storage location in Android.
